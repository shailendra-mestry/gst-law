Privacy Policy — GST law

App: com.gstlaw.app · Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Contact: aniketashokmestry@gmail.com

Signing in with Google is optional — the app works fully offline without it, exactly as before. If you don't sign in, the developer runs no server that stores your data, and uses no analytics or crash reporting; everything you create in the app stays on your device. If you do sign in, your bookmarks, notebook, reading history and settings sync to that Google account — see "Signing in and cloud sync" below. The other exception, whether or not you sign in, is advertising: the app shows banner ads through Google AdMob, and Google — as the advertising provider — collects some data to serve and measure those ads.

What stays on your device

Whether or not you sign in, the app stores the following only in its private storage on your own device, and never sends it to the developer:

  • your bookmarks, reading history and scroll positions;
  • your private notes and "client matter" tags (the Notebook);
  • your settings — theme, text size, and similar preferences;
  • your progress on checklists and flashcards.

If you've never signed in, none of this is linked to an account or any other person's data — there is no login, so there is nothing to link it to. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Signing in and cloud sync

Profile → Sign in with Google is entirely optional and shows what it does before you sign in. Signing in creates a Firebase Authentication account from your Google profile — it stores your name, email address and profile photo, your Google account ID, and an account ID this app's own Firebase project assigns you. Once signed in, the following syncs to that account and down to any other device you sign in on:

  • your bookmarks;
  • your notebook notes and client-matter names — these can include client-identifying research you've typed in, so treat sign-in like you would any other account that holds that kind of material;
  • your reading and tool history — recently viewed provisions and pinned tools (not your recent searches or recently-used-tools list, which stay device-only);
  • your reading-related settings — theme, density, spacing, margin, font, text alignment and similar display preferences (not text size, haptics or your push-notification choice, which also stay device-only).

This is stored in Cloud Firestore, inside this app's own Firebase project. Access rules restrict every other user to just their own data, never yours. The developer can technically access this database through the Firebase console, but does not look at it except to carry out a deletion request.

Signing out stops syncing only — it does not delete anything. The data already on your device stays, and so does the cloud copy, which keeps syncing to any other device still signed in. To remove the cloud copies for good, delete your account — see "Data deletion" below.

Advertising

The app displays banner ads on its browsing screens (the Library, Search, Tools and Saved tabs). Reading screens — provisions, judgments, circulars, notifications — carry no ads.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google collects information that may include your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate, city-level location is derived), device and app information, and limited data about your interaction with ads. Google uses this as an advertising partner; it is described in Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" and its AdMob data-disclosure.

Your choices:

  • Where the law requires a consent prompt (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows one on first launch and you can change your answer any time from Profile → Ad privacy choices. Declining means you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • On any Android device you can turn off ad personalisation and reset your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device), and review Google's ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

The app does not use AdMob's features for apps directed at children, and it is not directed at children (see below).

Other network requests the app makes

  • Content updates. The app periodically fetches updated reference data (new notifications, circulars, advisories and editorial notes) as a plain file download from a public GitHub Pages address. The request contains no information about you.
  • App software updates. The app uses Expo's over-the-air update service to check for a newer version of its own code. This check sends only technical details needed to serve the correct update — the device platform, the app and runtime version, and a randomly generated installation identifier that is not linked to your identity. It is not used for analytics, advertising, or profiling. Expo acts only as a service provider for this function; see Expo's privacy policy at expo.dev/privacy.
  • Links you choose to open. When you tap an external link (for example, a link to the official GST portal or a court judgment), your browser opens that site and its own privacy practices apply. The app does not send any of your data with these links.
  • Feedback. If you use a "send feedback" link, your own email app opens with a message pre-filled; nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.
  • Push notifications. If you turn on "What's new (push)" notifications, your device registers an anonymous push token — not linked to your identity or your Google account, if any — with this app's own Firebase project, used only to deliver that notification.

Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell your data and does not share it with anyone. If you don't sign in, the only data leaving your device is the advertising data collected by Google described above, the technical fields sent to Expo for update delivery, and your anonymous push token if notifications are on. If you sign in, your synced data (described above) also leaves your device, stored in Cloud Firestore under access rules that restrict it to your own signed-in account. All of these requests use HTTPS.

Data deletion

If you've never signed in, there is no account and no server-side copy — to remove everything the app has stored, uninstall it. If you've signed in, Profile → Account → Delete account in the app permanently deletes your synced bookmarks, notebook, history and settings from the cloud, and your Firebase account itself; local data on this device is kept. If you can't or don't want to open the app, email a deletion request to the contact below, or use the web deletion-request page. We aim to act on a verified deletion request within 30 days. To reset the advertising identifier used for ads, use your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Grievance / data contact (requests about your data, privacy, or a deletion request): Aniket Mestry — aniketashokmestry@gmail.com.

Permissions

The app requests only network access (to download content, app updates and ads) and vibration (for touch feedback). It does not request access to your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, files, or any other sensitive resource.

Children

The app is a professional and educational reference for tax law, is rated for a general audience, and is not directed at or designed to appeal to children.
