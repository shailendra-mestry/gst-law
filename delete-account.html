Delete your account and data — GST law

App: com.gstlaw.app, published by Aniket Mestry and Associates · Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Contact: aniketashokmestry@gmail.com

This page is for anyone who signed in to GST law with Google and wants their account and synced data deleted, whether or not they still have the app installed. See the full Privacy Policy for everything the app collects.

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open GST law and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm you're still signed in to the Google account when prompted, then confirm the deletion.

This immediately and permanently deletes your synced bookmarks, notebook, reading history and settings from our database, and your account record itself. Data already on that device stays on it — this only removes the account and its cloud copy.

Option 2: request deletion without the app

Email aniketashokmestry@gmail.com from the same email address your Google account uses to sign in, with the subject "Delete my GST law account", and we'll delete it on our end. We aim to act on a verified request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (name, email, profile photo on file with us, and its account ID).
  • Your synced bookmarks, notebook notes and client-matter names, reading and tool history, and settings — all four stored documents.

What is kept

Anything already saved on your own device — the app never had a reason to delete that, and account deletion doesn't touch it. If you'd also like that removed, uninstall the app or clear its storage from your device's app settings.

Grievance / data contact

For questions about this process, or any other request about your data or privacy: Aniket Mestry — aniketashokmestry@gmail.com.
